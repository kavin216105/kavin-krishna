About me

Curious about how things work, inside and out

I started with Python because it let me turn ideas into working programs quickly. Then I picked up a soldering iron, and I haven't put either down since.

I enjoy projects that need both sides: reading a sensor with a microcontroller, sending the data to a Python script, and turning it into something useful. I like debugging with a multimeter as much as with a print statement.

Outside of projects I read datasheets, take apart old gadgets, and learn something new about circuits or code most weeks.
